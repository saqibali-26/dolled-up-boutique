Our story

A family dream, all dolled up.

Dolled Up began in 2023 when Dorothy and her daughter turned a long-held love of fashion and home decor into a boutique. Today, the Cherry Hill store brings together women's fashion, children's clothing, accessories and home-inspired gifts — each selected with care.

It's a store built around making everyday life feel a little more beautiful, whether you're dressing yourself, your little one or someone you love.

xo, Dorothy
